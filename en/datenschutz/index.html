Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 20, 2026

Controller: Louis Maurice Dietl, contact: support@onstudio.de

Overview

The doify app does not collect any personal data for the provider. The provider runs no servers of its own for the app and receives none of your tasks, content or usage data.

Data storage

Your data is stored locally on your device and in your private iCloud via Apple CloudKit, so that it stays in sync across your devices. Only your Apple Account has access to this data. Apple processes it as a processor under the Apple Privacy Policy.

Notifications

Reminders are scheduled and shown locally on your device. No servers are used for this.

In-app purchases

Purchases of doify Pro (yearly subscription or lifetime) are handled by Apple through the App Store. The provider receives no payment or account data. The app only verifies the purchase locally on the device through Apple's StoreKit.

No ads, no tracking, no analytics

doify contains no ads, no tracking services, no analytics tools and no third-party software.

Your rights under the GDPR

You have the right to access, rectify and erase your data, and the right to lodge a complaint with a data protection supervisory authority. Since the provider does not store any personal data about you, you can exercise these rights regarding your tasks directly in the app and in your iCloud. If you have questions, write to support@onstudio.de.

Deletion

Uninstall the app to remove the local data. To remove the data in iCloud, go to Settings → Apple Account → iCloud → Manage Storage and delete the data of doify.

Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated, for example when new features are added. The current version is always available on this page.
