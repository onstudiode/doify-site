Support

Why don't my tasks show up on all devices?

Sync runs through iCloud and requires the same iCloud account on all devices. Also check that iCloud is turned on for doify and that the device is online.

How quickly does the Mac update?

The Mac updates every 10 seconds while a doify window is visible.

What is the “Recovered” column?

Tasks whose original column no longer exists, for example because it was deleted on another device, end up there. That way no task gets lost. You can move them from there to another column.

Reminders and notifications

Reminders appear as local notifications. This requires notification permission. You can change it in the system settings under Notifications → doify.

doify Pro

Recurring cards, checklists and widgets are part of doify Pro, as a yearly subscription or a one-time lifetime purchase. After reinstalling or on a new device, use “Restore Purchases” to restore your purchase. You can cancel the yearly subscription in your App Store account settings.
